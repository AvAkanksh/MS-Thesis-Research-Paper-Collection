### Top A* Ranked Conferences

These are premier conferences (A* in CORE rankings) where papers on Agent-Based Modeling (ABM) in finance are commonly published, particularly those involving multi-agent systems, AI applications, or computational simulations of financial markets. ABM papers often appear in tracks related to multi-agent systems, economic simulations, or AI-driven modeling.

| Conference Acronym | Full Name | Relevance to ABM in Finance |
|--------------------|-----------|-----------------------------|
| AAMAS | International Conference on Autonomous Agents and Multi-Agent Systems | Core venue for ABM research; frequently features papers on agent interactions in financial markets, market simulations, and economic agents. Includes workshops like MABS (Multi-Agent-Based Simulation) that directly address finance applications. |
| NeurIPS | Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems | Accepts ABM papers enhanced with machine learning, such as neural agents in financial forecasting or market behavior modeling. |
| ICML | International Conference on Machine Learning | Hosts workshops like AI4ABM, focusing on AI-integrated ABM for complex systems including finance; suitable for papers on learning agents in economic simulations. |
| AAAI | AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence | Broad AI scope includes ABM for decision-making in finance, such as agent-based risk assessment or behavioral economics models. |
| IJCAI | International Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence | Features ABM in areas like game theory and multi-agent coordination, applicable to financial trading agents and market dynamics. |

### Top A Ranked Conferences

These A-ranked conferences (per CORE) also accept ABM papers in finance, often in simulation, computational economics, or interdisciplinary tracks. They provide strong venues for applied ABM work in financial sectors.

| Conference Acronym | Full Name | Relevance to ABM in Finance |
|--------------------|-----------|-----------------------------|
| WSC | Winter Simulation Conference | Dedicated track on Agent-Based Simulation (ABS); ideal for ABM in financial scenarios like market crises, trading simulations, and risk modeling. |
| ACM EC | ACM Conference on Economics and Computation | Focuses on computational economics; accepts ABM for modeling auctions, markets, and financial networks. |
| ECAI | European Conference on Artificial Intelligence | Includes ABM in multi-agent and simulation tracks, with applications to economic and financial agent interactions. |
| ICAIF | International Conference on AI in Finance | Emerging venue for AI-driven ABM in finance, such as DeFi simulations and market prediction; while newer, it aligns with A-level impact in applied AI. |
| CEF | Computing in Economics and Finance | Strong emphasis on computational methods in economics; ABM is a dominant topic here for finance-related models like macroeconomic forecasting.
